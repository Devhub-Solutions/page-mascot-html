The smallest possible mascot

Three things on the page: an element to mount into, the library, and one call. The character follows your cursor, blinks when you click, and gets dizzy after four quick pokes. 

<div id="mascot-host"></div>
<script src="page-mascot.js"></script>
<script>
  PageMascot.createMascot(document.getElementById('mascot-host'), {
    directions: 'mascots/fox-directions.webp',
    reactions:  'mascots/fox-reactions.webp',
    size: 160,
    label: 'fox'
  });
</script>
